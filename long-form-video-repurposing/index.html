Long-Form Video Repurposing for Courses and Webinars

Turn detailed teaching and webinar recordings into useful discovery content.

Step-by-step workflow

  1. Split the recording into one concept per clip.
  2. Show the practical answer early, then retain the supporting explanation.
  3. Add captions and mobile-friendly framing for silent viewing.
  4. Use clips as previews that lead viewers to the complete lesson or webinar.
