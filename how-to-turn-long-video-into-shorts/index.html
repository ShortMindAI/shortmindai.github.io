How to Turn a Long Video into Shorts

A practical workflow for turning podcasts, interviews, webinars and livestreams into focused vertical clips.

Step-by-step workflow

  1. Choose a source with clear chapters or recurring topics.
  2. Use AI highlight detection to shortlist moments with a complete idea.
  3. Rewrite the first seconds as a direct hook, then add readable captions.
  4. Reframe the speaker or subject for 9:16 and export versions for each platform.
