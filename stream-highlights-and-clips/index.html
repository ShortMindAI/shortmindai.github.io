How to Create Stream Highlights and Stream Clips

Recover the best gameplay, reactions and turning points from long livestream replays.

Step-by-step workflow

  1. Process the replay while the stream is still topical.
  2. Collect wins, fails, reactions and audience questions in one review list.
  3. Use vertical framing and captions for viewers watching on mobile.
  4. Publish several clips with different moments instead of one long recap.
